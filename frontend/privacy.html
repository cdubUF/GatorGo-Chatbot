Privacy

Microphone, camera and spoken replies

The microphone and camera start off and require separate permission. Recording stops after 30 seconds. Audio is sent to this server and OpenAI for transcription only when you stop a recording. Cancel discards unsent audio. Server audio is held in memory during the request and is not written to disk. Cancelling an upload cannot retract audio already received by the provider. Voice questions send automatically by default. Uncheck Send when recording stops to review the transcript first. Start conversation records each turn, sends after a pause, and listens again after the AI finishes speaking. End conversation stops the loop.

The camera is a self-preview only. Camera frames are attached directly to the local video element; they are not uploaded. Turning the camera off, changing guides, hiding the tab or leaving the page stops its tracks.

Spoken replies send the visible answer to OpenAI to create an AI-generated voice. Audio is returned through this server and played in your browser; it is not written to disk. Stop audio or uncheck AI voice to mute. Reduced-motion mode disables idle movement and blinking.

Only your guide choice is saved in this tab's session storage. No camera, microphone or chat content is stored there.

Do not enter payment cards, identity documents, health information, real booking details, or addresses. The reservation form is for fictional test data only.

The browser keeps your conversation and session token in memory for this tab. The server stores bounded topic context and generated replies in memory for up to 30 minutes of inactivity by default. Restarting the server clears sessions.

For AI responses, the server sends your question, limited context, and relevant project knowledge to OpenAI. After you verify a test reservation, it also sends the hotel name, stay dates, room type, guest count, and status while the reservation is connected. It does not send the guest name, confirmation number, or verification code as reservation context. Disconnect it or start a new conversation to stop sharing. Automated detection of sensitive information is imperfect.

Handoff summaries are displayed to you but are not sent to staff. The application does not make bookings, refunds, charges, or issue keys.

This service runs locally and is not yet configured for public deployment or real staff operations.
