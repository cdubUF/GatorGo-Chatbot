Privacy

Microphone, camera and spoken replies

The microphone and camera start off and require separate permission. Recording stops after 30 seconds. Audio is sent to this server and OpenAI for transcription only when you stop a recording. Cancel discards unsent audio. Server audio is held in memory during the request and is not written to disk. Cancelling an upload cannot retract audio already received by the provider. Voice questions send automatically by default. Uncheck Send when recording stops to review the transcript first. Talk to Gator or Start call keeps microphone capture active. Voice interruption is enabled by default: speak over a reply or use Interrupt to stop it. Disable Voice interruption if speaker echo causes false triggers. Mute mic disables the current microphone track until you unmute. In live Realtime mode, microphone audio streams directly to OpenAI over an encrypted WebRTC connection while the call is active; it does not wait for a pause to upload. The server provides the sample resort knowledge and session instructions. Spoken questions and answers appear in the conversation in this tab. They are cleared by New conversation or reloading; they are not saved to browser storage. In the legacy turn mode, each detected turn, up to 30 seconds, is sent after a pause. End call stops capture, queued playback, and pending requests. Call turns preserve your typed draft and add one transcript entry per speaker. A call ends automatically after ten minutes.

The camera is a self-preview only. Camera frames are attached directly to the local video element; they are not uploaded. Turning the camera off, changing guides, hiding the tab or leaving the page stops its tracks.

Spoken replies send the visible answer to OpenAI to create an AI-generated voice. For recorded or typed replies, audio is returned through this server; live Realtime replies stream directly from OpenAI to the browser. This app does not write call audio to disk. Stop audio or uncheck AI voice to mute. Reduced-motion mode disables idle movement and blinking.

Only your theme and color-mode preferences are saved in browser local storage. No guest conversation or trip details are stored there.

Optional feedback stores aggregate reason counts by source ID in server memory, without your message text or reservation details. One feedback submission is allowed per reply. Session plans stay in browser memory and clear on a new conversation or reload. Call timing reports contain numeric timing checkpoints and turn status, without microphone recordings or transcripts. Downloading a timing report saves it only when requested. Only your guide choice is saved in this tab's session storage. No camera, microphone or chat content is stored there.

Do not enter payment cards, identity documents, health information, real booking details, or addresses. The reservation form is for fictional test data only.

The browser keeps your conversation and session token in memory for this tab. Text and transcribed voice turns share one server session. The server stores a limited window of screened questions and replies, plus a short summary of topics and preferences, in memory for up to 30 minutes of inactivity by default. Detected sensitive details and instruction overrides are omitted; automated screening is imperfect. Spoken assistant wording is not retained as verified facts. Voice synchronization uses a temporary token held only in this tab. New conversation clears the shared session. Resort lookup tools read supplied knowledge; handoff preparation creates a reviewable summary and does not notify staff. Restarting the server clears sessions.

For AI responses, the server sends your question, limited context, and relevant project knowledge to OpenAI. After you verify a test reservation, it also sends the hotel name, stay dates, room type, guest count, and status while the reservation is connected. It does not send the guest name, confirmation number, or verification code as reservation context. Disconnect it or start a new conversation to stop sharing. Automated detection of sensitive information is imperfect.

Handoff summaries are displayed to you but are not sent to staff. The application does not make bookings, refunds, charges, or issue keys.

This service runs locally and is not yet configured for public deployment or real staff operations.
