Data sources

The concierge answers from a small, fictional sample knowledge base. The following datasets were collected or profiled as background and possible evaluation material. They are not currently used to train the model or automatically answer guest questions.

Downloaded conversation datasets

  • Bitext Hospitality — 25,000 examples of hotel questions and responses.
  • Bitext Customer Support — 26,872 customer-service examples.
  • MultiWOZ 2.1 — travel and hotel task-oriented conversations.

Supplied background files

  • export.csv — 1,393 rental-permit records.
  • hotel_bookings.csv and archive (9).zip / hotel_booking.csv — two supplied copies of historical hotel-booking data, each with 119,390 records; they are not assumed to be independent datasets.

These sources do not establish Gator Go hotel policies. Source details and licenses are documented in the project repository.
